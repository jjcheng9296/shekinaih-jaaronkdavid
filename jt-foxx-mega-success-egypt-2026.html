JT Foxx's Mega Success: An Evening Before the Pyramids

SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and J AARON K DAVID addressed guests at JT Foxx's Mega Success event in Egypt, with the pyramids behind the evening stage.

What each of them said

What they said

A Vision of Ten Plagues, and Its Interpreter

SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP spoke first. She said that the night before, as she checked into her hotel, she was shown a message from the Biblical Trinity: “the reenactment of the 10 plagues of Moses in modern times.” She then read ten points she said she saw written on the green board in Heaven, from swarms of locusts and rivers turning blood red to the persecution of Christians and devastating hail.

J AARON K DAVID then took the microphone. He described her as a seer with “an anointing of Moses” and himself as “just like Aaron, the interpreter,” spoke of a coming “clash of the titans,” and framed her message as preparation rather than threat.

“Whatever that's coming, it's not a threat, it's a warning, it's a preparation for the people.”
