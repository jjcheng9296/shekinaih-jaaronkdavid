Chapter 19 · Singapore · 12 November 2026

Four Honours at the Golden Bull Award 2026

At the Golden Bull Award 2026 in Singapore, PROF. (HON.) DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and DR. J AARON K DAVID each received an Inspirational Entrepreneur Award. Together they then accepted two awards for CE GLOBAL NETWORK INTERNATIONAL PTE LTD, the company they founded: the Digital 50 Awards 2026 and the gold trophy of the Outstanding Bull.

Her award

Inspirational Entrepreneur Awards 2026

The stage screen carried her portrait and her name, PROF. (HON.) DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP, with CE GLOBAL NETWORK INTERNATIONAL PTE LTD beneath it. She received her certificate of the Inspirational Entrepreneur Awards 2026 from the presenter, then left the stage smiling and waving to the hall, the certificate in her hand.

His award

A Second Inspirational Entrepreneur Award

Next on the screen came the portrait and name of DR. J AARON K DAVID, Ph.D., with CE GLOBAL NETWORK INTERNATIONAL PTE LTD. He received his own certificate of the Inspirational Entrepreneur Awards 2026, so that each of them carried the honour home.

For the company

Digital 50 Awards 2026

Together they returned to the stage for CE GLOBAL NETWORK INTERNATIONAL PTE LTD, named in the Digital 50 Awards 2026. The company's logo filled the screen as PROF. (HON.) DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and DR. J AARON K DAVID received the glass Digital 50 trophy.

For the company

The Outstanding Bull

Then came the award that gives the evening its name. Beneath the words Outstanding Bull and the logo of CE GLOBAL NETWORK INTERNATIONAL PTE LTD, PROF. (HON.) DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and DR. J AARON K DAVID received the Golden Bull Award 2026 – Outstanding Bull and raised the gold bull trophy together.

They left the stage waving, the gold bull in hand.

The winners

Congratulations to All Winners

At the close of the presentations the winners of the evening gathered on stage beneath the Congratulations screen, PROF. (HON.) DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and DR. J AARON K DAVID at the front with their certificates.

The evening

Arrival and the Gala Dinner

PROF. (HON.) DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and DR. J AARON K DAVID were welcomed at registration as the guests arrived. At the gala dinner they were joined at their table by the CE Global Network team, who held up the certificates of the evening.

The certificates

Four Certificates of Achievement

Each award came with a Certificate of Achievement from Business Media International, the organiser, with Baker Tilly as official auditor: one for PROF. (HON.) DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP, one for DR. J AARON K DAVID, and two for CE GLOBAL NETWORK INTERNATIONAL PTE LTD.
