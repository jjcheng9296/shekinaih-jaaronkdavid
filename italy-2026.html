An Italian Chapter
From the Gallery to Maranello

In August 2026, SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and J AARON K DAVID joined JT Foxx's international event in Italy, held at the Lamborghini museum before a gathering of leaders and entrepreneurs from many nations. There, SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP addressed the room with J AARON K DAVID at her side; and at Museo Ferrari in Maranello, among the racing cars, she later shared a word of blessing with the guests gathered for the evening.

★ At the Lamborghini Museum · The Address

Before Leaders and Entrepreneurs from Around the World

The day-long event, hosted by JT Foxx at the Lamborghini museum alongside members of the Lamborghini family, brought together leaders and entrepreneurs from many nations. Introduced from the stage together with J AARON K DAVID, SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP took the microphone on the museum floor, among classic cars and archival portraits — and addressed the guests, in the host's words, "distinguished guests, leaders, entrepreneurs from around the world."

The Address

Two Organisations, One Purpose of Service

SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP introduced herself and her ministry and business partner, J AARON K DAVID, and the two organisations they represent: CE Global International, a Singapore-based international freight-forwarding company, and Crowned Eagles Global Heaven Throne Room Ministry.

She went on to set out what she described as seven areas of service — the first, to stand as "a bridge between heaven and earth"; the second, to bring "heaven's answer and solution to individuals, families, business, communities, governments and nations."

"It is a great honor for me to stand before you in Lamborghini, Italy today."

SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP · Opening words of her address · Italy · August 2026
01 The Setting · A Museum Built Around a Legacy

Among the Classics

The address was given on the museum floor itself — beneath a large archival portrait, beside a silver Lamborghini 350 GTV on display, with the audience seated among the exhibits.

J AARON K DAVID stood beside SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP throughout the address, as the two were received by an audience drawn from many nations.
