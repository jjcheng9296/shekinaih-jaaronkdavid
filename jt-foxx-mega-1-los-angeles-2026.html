The seven missions

To Alert and Warn Humanity

PROF. (HON.) DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP opened with one of the seven functions of CROWNED EAGLES GLOBAL and Heaven Throne Room Ministry: “to alert and warn humanity and provide a way of escape”.

She then named the seven missions in turn: an advocate between Heaven, earth and humanity; Heaven's messenger; Heaven's warning; Heaven's restoration, which brings healings, miracles and deliverance; Heaven's decree for nations and leaders; Heaven's solution to humanity's greatest challenges; and Heaven's eternal destiny imparter.

For the USA and the world

Seven Things

Speaking of the airline disruption in the USA on 13 November 2026, she called it “a wake-up call to alert people in USA and worldwide to buckle up”, and set out the seven things she saw the GOD of the Universe place on the green board in Heaven for the USA and the world at large: seasons of testing for the USA, extreme weather, the financial sector, political instability, World War III and World War IV, famine worldwide, and number seven: “when all else fail, remember, try the GOD of the Universe”.

Her ministry partner

GOD Cares for You

DR. J AARON K DAVID closed with why GOD gave this message: “because HE cares for HIS men, for HIS creations, regardless of race or religion”.

“GOD is not blind, GOD is not deaf, neither is HIS hand too short to come to your aid,” he said. “GOD is merciful, GOD is long-suffering, GOD is full of compassion and HE loves you.”
