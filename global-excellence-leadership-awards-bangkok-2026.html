Her award

Global Entrepreneurial Leadership Icon

The screen above the stage carried her portrait and the full title of the award: Global Entrepreneurial Leadership Icon & Divine Counsel Excellence Award for Spiritual Leadership, Supernatural & Ultimate Solutions for Humanity 2026, awarded to PROF. (HON.) DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP.

Called to the stage by name, PROF. (HON.) DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP received her award with a handshake and raised it to the hall. Invited to share a word, she began with thanks: “I thank the God of the Universe that every achievement without Him, nothing is impossible.”

She described the last 30 years as a life given over entirely: “So I'm a steward.” Every recognition, she said, carries a purpose, “because the voice need to speak loud, louder and loudest, so that the entire earth can be safe.”

His award

Divine Understanding, Theological Wisdom and Transformational Leadership

Next on the screen came his portrait and the title Global Excellence Award for Divine Understanding, Theological Wisdom & Transformational Leadership Excellence 2026, awarded to DR. J AARON K DAVID, Ph.D. (Theology and Divine Understanding).

DR. J AARON K DAVID received his award with a handshake and spoke of why the two of them had come: “Because it seems that everybody has the opinion that God is dead.” He closed with words for every nation and government: “They can despise, they can scoff, they can mock, but God has the last laugh.”
