An Evening to Remember in Kuala Lumpur

The Arrival

Red Carpet at The Majestic Hotel

On 22 October 2026 DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and DR. J AARON K DAVID arrived at The Majestic Hotel Kuala Lumpur and walked the red carpet past the banners of The BrandLaureate BestBrands Awards 2026.

Reception

Their Names in the Guestbook

At the reception table DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and DR. J AARON K DAVID signed the guestbook, and the two sat side by side for an interview at the hotel.

Her Award

Visionary Brand ICON Takes the Stage

The World Brands Foundation presented The BrandLaureate Brand ICON Leadership Award 2026 to DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP as Visionary Brand ICON for Enduring Business Stewardship and Humanitarian Impact. Tun Dato' Seri Md Raus Sharif, former Chief Justice of Malaysia and Patron of The BrandLaureate, handed her the trophy, and Prof. Dr. KKJohan, Miss Universe Malaysia and the presenters stood with her as she raised it.

His Award

Corporate Icon for Transformational Leadership

DR. J AARON K DAVID received The BrandLaureate Brand ICON Leadership Award 2026 as Corporate Icon for Transformational Leadership & Purpose. Tun Md Raus Sharif handed him the trophy with a handshake, with Prof. Dr. KKJohan, Miss Universe Malaysia and the presenters on stage beside him.

After the Ceremony

One Trophy Held High Together

After the ceremony DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and DR. J AARON K DAVID stood before the illuminated awards wall and lifted a gold winged trophy between them.

With the Hosts

Welcomed by The BrandLaureate Family

DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and DR. J AARON K DAVID spent time with Prof. Dr. KKJohan, World President of The World Brands Foundation, Hilary Ng, Chief Executive Officer of The BrandLaureate, Miss Universe Malaysia, a key member of The BrandLaureate team and Alain Robert, the French Spider-Man.

Friends of the Evening

Toasts, Laughter and Warm Embraces

DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP raised a glass and shared laughter with guests at the awards wall, and Florence, a successful Malaysian entrepreneur and a key figure of the evening, posed before the trophies.

The Team

Thumbs Up from the Whole Team

The CE Global Network International and Crowned Eagles Global team gathered around DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and DR. J AARON K DAVID at the sponsors' wall, the BestBrands backdrop and the awards wall, joined by Hilary Ng, Alain Robert, Miss Universe Malaysia and Florence.

The Class of 2026

All the 2026 Award Recipients

An official collage gathers the award recipients of The BrandLaureate BestBrands Awards 2026 on stage, DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and DR. J AARON K DAVID among them.
