Two Brand ICON Leadership Awards in Kuala Lumpur

DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP received The BrandLaureate Brand ICON Leadership Award 2026 as Visionary Brand ICON: Enduring Business Stewardship and Humanitarian Impact, and DR. J AARON K DAVID received it as Corporate Icon: Transformational Leadership & Purpose. The World Brands Foundation presented both awards at The Majestic Hotel Kuala Lumpur on 22 October 2026, with its World President, Prof. Dr. KKJohan, on stage. Afterwards the two spoke to camera beside the awards wall and sat together for a recipient interview.

Her award

Visionary Brand ICON

DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP was called to the stage first. The screen above the stage carried her name, her role as Founder & Managing Partner of CE Global Network International Pte Ltd and her official portrait, beneath the words Visionary Brand ICON, Enduring Business Stewardship and Humanitarian Impact.

Tun Dato' Seri Md Raus Sharif, former Chief Justice of Malaysia and Patron of The BrandLaureate, handed her the gold winged trophy with a handshake while another presenter held up her framed certificate, Prof. Dr. KKJohan at their side. She raised the trophy high in her left hand and held the certificate in her right as the stage party gave a thumbs-up and applauded on either side. Framed in gold on a red mat, the certificate carries her name, her company and the full title of her award above the signatures and a gold seal.

His award

Corporate Icon

DR. J AARON K DAVID followed about a minute later. His screen read Corporate Icon, Transformational Leadership & Purpose, above his name, his role as Co-founder & Partner of CE Global Network International Pte Ltd and his official portrait.

He took the trophy from Tun Md Raus Sharif with a handshake, Prof. Dr. KKJohan again beside the presenters, and laughed as a presenter passed him the framed certificate. Then he lifted the trophy at centre stage to the same thumbs-up and applause. Within minutes, the two partners of CE Global Network International Pte Ltd each held a Brand ICON Leadership Award.

The awards

One Award, Two Distinctions

The World Brands Foundation presents The BrandLaureate Brand ICON Leadership Award to what its 2026 release calls “distinguished leaders, visionaries and changemakers.” This year two of those leaders came from the same Singapore company, and each received the award in a category of its own: DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP as Visionary Brand ICON and DR. J AARON K DAVID as Corporate Icon.

The Foundation describes her category, Visionary Brand ICON, as recognising leadership that reaches beyond corporate achievement into enduring stewardship, broader influence and humanitarian or societal impact, and ranks it as the higher distinction of the two. In the Foundation’s terms, his category, Corporate Icon, recognises distinguished leadership and transformational contribution within the corporate and professional sphere.

In the Foundation’s release on the gala, Prof. Dr. KKJohan, its World President, set the spirit of the evening: “Every great achievement begins with the courage to take the first step,” he said, and spoke of the confidence to believe in what is possible and the conviction to persevere. The theme of The BrandLaureate BestBrands Awards 2026 was “The Courage to Challenge.”

DR. SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP and DR. J AARON K DAVID arrived at The Majestic Hotel Kuala Lumpur on the evening of 22 October 2026 for The BrandLaureate Awards Gala Dinner, presented by The World Brands Foundation. They stepped in from the street through the hotel’s tall glass doors and walked the red carpet across the marble lobby, past the banner for The BrandLaureate BestBrands Awards 2026 and its theme, “The Courage to Challenge.”

She wore a silver beaded gown with crystal fringe on the shoulder straps and star-shaped pins in her hair; he wore a dark navy suit, white shirt and red tie. Each wore a pink rose corsage.

On the way in they signed the guestbook at the reception table, posed together at the official backdrop and between the banners of the BestBrands and SMEs BestBrands Awards, and walked a corridor hung with large paintings.
