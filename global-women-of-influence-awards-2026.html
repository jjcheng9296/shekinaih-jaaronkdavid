She thanked her husband, PEOW, for releasing her to serve the God of the Universe; her daughter, Jessica, for her understanding, patience and support; her business and ministry partner J AARON K DAVID, “for serving faithfully beside me”; and every member of Crowned Eagles Global and CE Global Network International.

Seven blessings

“May You Rise. May You Break Through.”

She closed by releasing seven blessings over “the women in this room and women worldwide”:

  1. courage, bravery and endurance to continue their work;
  2. wisdom from above for every responsibility;
  3. divine energy, strength and passion;
  4. discernment to make the right choices in each season;
  5. breakthrough over every obstacle and barrier;
  6. strength, well-being and excellent health;
  7. a spirit of excellence in everything they put their hands to.
